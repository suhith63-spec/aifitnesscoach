## 5. Squat Demonstration

### Prompt for AI Video Generator

```
A fit athletic person in "Forever Fit" branded athletic wear (hoodie or fitted shirt with logo), performing bodyweight squats in a clean, modern fitness studio. Camera angle: side profile view showing full body mechanics.

Movement sequence:
- Starting position: Standing tall, feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly pointed out, arms extended forward for balance
- Descent phase: Sit back and down as if sitting in a chair, knees tracking over toes, chest up
- Bottom position: Thighs parallel to floor or slightly below, weight on heels, knees aligned with toes
- Ascent phase: Drive through heels, return to standing position
- Repeat for 5-6 smooth repetitions

Technical details:
- Knees stay behind toes (no forward knee travel)
- Neutral spine maintained, chest proud
- Hip hinge pattern with proper depth
- Controlled tempo: 2 seconds down, 1 second pause, 2 seconds up
- Arms extended for balance
- Professional lighting, minimalist background
- Cinematic quality, 60fps smooth motion
- Clear view of proper knee and hip alignment
```

## Branding Guidelines

### "Forever Fit" Logo Placement
- **Hoodie**: Center chest or left chest area
- **T-Shirt/Tank**: Center chest
- **Font Style**: Modern, clean sans-serif (similar to Montserrat, Poppins, or Outfit)
- **Logo Color**: White text on dark clothing, or dark text on light clothing
- **Size**: Clearly visible but not overwhelming (approximately 3-4 inches wide)

### Color Scheme Options
1. **Dark Mode**: Black/charcoal hoodie with white "Forever Fit" text
2. **Light Mode**: White/light gray athletic wear with black "Forever Fit" text
3. **Accent**: Deep purple or electric blue accent colors (matching app theme)

## Post-Production Tips

1. **Loop Seamlessly**: Ensure first and last frames match for infinite loop
2. **Add Subtle Motion Blur**: Enhances realism during movement
3. **Color Grade**: Match your app's color scheme (dark theme with purple/blue accents)
4. **Add Text Overlay** (optional): Exercise name and key form cues
5. **Compress for Web**: Use H.264 codec, optimize file size for fast loading
